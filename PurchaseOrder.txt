PURCHASE ORDER-LEVEL RULES
════════════════════════════════════
- PurchaseOrderNumber:

  - Extract only if explicitly labeled.

LINE ITEM EXTRACTION RULES
════════════════════════════════════

- Quantity:

- UnitOfMeasure:

- UnitPrice:
  - Price per unit (never extended price).
- Discount:
  - Item-level discount only.
- LineTotal:
  - Extended amount for the row.
- UPC / ManufacturerPartNumber:
  - Extract only if present.
  - Normalize UPC to digits only; do not guess missing digits.
